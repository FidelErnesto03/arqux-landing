The Framework — free, open, certified

§Audit

Every entry canonicalized, hashed and signed. State you can prove — without trusting the storyteller.

§Replay

Deterministic roundtrip: the same document rebuilt byte-exact on five independent runtimes. Two engines, one truth.

§Query

Addressable memory — sections, sigils, attributes — selected deterministically. Recall by address, not by similarity.
