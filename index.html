THREE VERBS, ONE SUBSTRATE

The verbs are the product.

§

Audit

Every entry canonicalized, hashed, chained and signed. State you can prove — to a regulator, a counterparty, or your own team at 3am.

§

Replay

Deterministic roundtrip: the same document rebuilt byte-exact on five independent runtimes. Two engines, one truth. Point-in-time state, reconstructable.

§

Query

Addressable memory — sections, sigils, attributes — selected deterministically. Recall by address, not by similarity. No statistical guessing about what your agent knew.
