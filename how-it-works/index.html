Learning

Learning is governed; its routes differ.

Lessons are recorded in the project brain. Elevating a lesson to an identity is a separate, explicit step: the inspected path requires a dry-run proposal and hash confirmation. Other promotion routes can run under a configured policy; they do not follow the same path.

Lesson recorded→Proposal reviewed→Criterion available
  • 01RecordA lesson is recorded in the project brain.
  • 02ReviewA proposal is reviewed before any identity change.
  • 03ApplyOn this route, applying requires the reviewed hash; policy may automate other routes.

In the home example, the error-message decision is the lesson recorded here; review decides whether it becomes a criterion.
